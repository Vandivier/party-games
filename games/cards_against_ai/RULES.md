# Cards Against AI

**Props:** two custom decks — Input Cards and Output Cards. No poker deck, no dice.

A party game for 3–8 players, inspired by Cards Against Humanity. You are the
model. Somebody out there is typing at you, and you have to answer.

## Rules (as authored)

- The game does not use poker cards. It uses two custom decks: "Input Cards" and
  "Output Cards".
- 3–8 players recommended.
- To start, players draw five Output Cards to their hand.
- Each round, an "Input Card" is drawn, representing a question, input, or prompt
  from a human. These should emphasize funny and dumb questions as well as some
  totally mundane, boring, or academic questions sprinkled in.
- Turns are simultaneous. Each player plays an "Output Card" representing the
  answer they would give the end user's Input Card.
- Cards are played face down. Once every player has a face down Output Card, then
  the cards are shuffled and revealed.
- Players then discuss who has the best or funniest output card and vote on their
  favorite. Players cannot vote for their own card.
- When the voting period concludes there will either be a single most-voted card
  or a tie among winners. Each round winner collects a point.
- When a player has seven points, the game ends and they are declared the winner.

## Notes

The rules above are the authoritative rules of the game. Points they leave open
(hand refills, deck exhaustion, what two players should do about voting) are
settled for table play and for this repo's implementation in
[HOUSE_RULES.md](./HOUSE_RULES.md).
