# Hero War — House Rules

[RULES.md](./RULES.md) is authoritative. This file fills the gaps it leaves open
so the game can be played (and implemented) without arguing. Every entry below is
a default choice, not part of the authored rules.

## Cards and values

- Card value: A = 1, 2–10 = pip value, J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13.
- Face cards are J, Q, and K. Aces are not face cards.
- A face card of clubs (J♣, Q♣, K♣) may be played either as a hero or as
  equipment — the player choosing to play it decides which.
- Decks used: one standard 52-card deck for 2–3 players, two decks for 4+.

## Heroes

- A hero's face value is both its damage and its hit points: J = 11, Q = 12,
  K = 13. A Jack deals 11 and dies to 11 damage.
- Damage accumulates. A hero that has taken damage stays damaged across turns;
  nothing heals a hero (hearts nullify attacks, they do not restore hit points).
- Because a bare hero already deals 11–13, most clean hits kill outright. Hearts
  are the real defense; equipment decides who wins the trades that hearts do not
  stop.
- A hero dies when its hit points reach 0.
- A replacement hero is played immediately when the old one dies. It enters at
  full hit points and does not consume the player's play action for the turn.
- Equipment belongs to the player, not the hero: clubs stay equipped when a hero
  dies and boost the replacement hero.
- A player with no hero in play cannot attack.

## Setup

- Each player draws five cards. A player whose opening hand holds no face card
  shows the hand to the opponents, returns it to the deck, shuffles, and draws
  five again. The mulligan applies to the opening hand only.
- Players play a hero from their opening hand before the first turn.
- The first player is the first seat unless the table agrees otherwise.

## Turn structure

On your turn, in any order:

1. **Draw** — draw one card. Once per turn.
2. **Play** — play one card: a hero (only if you have none in play) or a club as
   equipment. Once per turn.
3. **Spade actions** — any number, each discarding one spade (see below).
4. **Attack** — attack once, or pass. Equipping a club and attacking are mutually
   exclusive on the same turn: whichever you do first rules the other out. This
   applies to equipment only — fielding a hero, drawing, and spade actions leave
   your attack intact.

Ending your turn passes play to the next living player.

## Damage

- Attack damage = hero's face value + the value of every club equipped + the
  value of every diamond discarded to boost this attack.
- A club adds its own value as a permanent damage bonus (A♣ = +1 … K♣ = +13).
- A diamond adds its own value for one attack only. Any number of diamonds may
  boost a single attack.

## Defense

- After the attacker declares the attack and its total damage, the defender may
  discard one heart to nullify it entirely. One heart is enough; a second does
  nothing.
- A nullified attack deals no damage. The attack action is still spent.

## Spades

Discarding a spade does one of:

- **Sabotage** — destroy one club equipped by an opponent of your choice. The
  destroyed club goes to the discard pile.
- **Trade** — draw one card. This does not consume the turn's draw or play
  action, so you may still draw and play normally the same turn.

## Table upkeep

- When the deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck. Cards in
  hand, heroes in play, and equipped clubs are not shuffled in.
- If the deck and the discard pile are both empty, draws simply produce nothing.
- There is no hand-size limit.

## Multiplayer

- With three or more players, the attacker names the target of each attack.
- A player is out when their hero dies and their hand holds no face card. Their
  hand and equipment go to the discard pile. The last player standing wins.
